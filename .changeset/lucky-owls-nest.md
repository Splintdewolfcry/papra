Tags can now be organized in hierarchies using `/` in their name (`LA/School` is the `School` tag nested under `LA`) and can be hidden with the new visibility option, both from the tags settings page and directly from a tag picker. Hidden tags are no longer suggested in the tag pickers, and documents tagged with a hidden tag are hidden from the document list for everyone in the organization.
